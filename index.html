Privacy Policy — damarcand apps

This policy applies to all apps published by damarcand on Google Play.

// data collection

damarcand apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All data you enter in our apps — profiles, settings, or any other content — is stored exclusively on your own device and never sent anywhere.

// third-party services

Our apps do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services of any kind.

// permissions

Any permissions requested by our apps are used solely to provide app functionality and never for data collection purposes.

// changes

If this policy is ever updated, the new version will be published at this page. We don't anticipate significant changes, given our commitment to not collecting data in the first place.
